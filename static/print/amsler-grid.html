Amsler grid

  1. Wear your reading glasses and hold the grid at reading distance in good light.
  2. Cover one eye.
  3. Look at the center dot and keep looking at it.
  4. While looking at the dot, check that all the lines are straight and all the squares are there.
  5. Repeat with the other eye. Check once or twice a week.

Call the same day if lines look wavy, bent or broken, or a blurred or missing patch appears, and it is new.
