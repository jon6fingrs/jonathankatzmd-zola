One page each, written to be printed and handed out, or kept on the refrigerator. Each has a QR code that opens the full page, with its animation. They are general information; your own doctor's instructions come first.

Cataract Surgery Handout

The cloudy natural lens is replaced with a clear lens implant, as an outpatient, usually in under half an hour.

YAG Capsulotomy Handout

A quick office laser that clears the cloudy capsule behind a lens implant after cataract surgery.

SLT Laser Handout

An office laser that lowers eye pressure by treating the eye's drain. It takes a few minutes, with no cuts or stitches.

Laser Iridotomy Handout

An office laser that makes a tiny opening in the iris to open a narrow drainage angle and prevent an angle-closure attack.

MIGS Handout

Small glaucoma procedures, often done with cataract surgery, that improve the eye's own drainage with a quicker recovery than traditional surgery.

Trabeculectomy Handout

Glaucoma surgery that makes a new drain under the upper eyelid, for pressure that drops and laser cannot control.

Tube Shunt Surgery Handout

A small tube and plate that carry fluid out of the eye, for glaucoma that needs surgery.

Micropulse and CPC Laser Handout

A laser applied through the white of the eye that lowers pressure by reducing how much fluid the eye makes.

The information and tools on this site are for general education and convenience. They are not medical advice and do not replace the instructions you receive from your own doctor.
