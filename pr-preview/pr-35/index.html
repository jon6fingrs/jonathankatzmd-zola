About Dr. Katz

Dr. Jonathan Katz is a fellowship-trained glaucoma specialist and comprehensive ophthalmologist who joined Katzen Eye Group in 2019. He treats primary and secondary glaucoma medically and surgically, from laser and minimally invasive procedures to trabeculectomy and tube shunt surgery, and performs cataract surgery. He trained at George Washington University and completed his residency and glaucoma fellowship at the Emory Eye Center.
