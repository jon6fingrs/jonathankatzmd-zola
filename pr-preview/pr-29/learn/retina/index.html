Learn›Retina

The retina is the light-sensing layer at the back of the eye. These pages explain the retinal conditions seen most often, which symptoms are urgent, and what treatment involves. Retinal surgery and injections are done by a retina specialist; your eye doctor will refer you when one is needed.

Conditions

Flashes and Floaters

Why floaters and flashes appear, what happens when the gel inside the eye pulls away from the retina, and the warning signs that need a same-day exam.

Retinal Tears and Detachment

How a retinal tear leads to a detachment, the warning signs, how a tear is sealed with laser, and how a detached retina is repaired. With an animation of both.

Macular Degeneration

Dry and wet macular degeneration: what each does to vision, AREDS2 vitamins, a printable Amsler grid for home checks, and treatment with injections.

Diabetic Eye Disease

How diabetes damages the retina, why early stages have no symptoms, how often to be examined, and how retinopathy and macular swelling are treated.

Treatment

Injections into the Eye

What to expect from an injection into the eye for wet macular degeneration, diabetic swelling or a vein blockage: the visit, aftercare, risks, how often.

Vitrectomy

Vitrectomy (PPV): surgery to remove the gel inside the eye to clear blood, repair a detachment or macular hole, or remove scar tissue. What recovery involves.

Laser for the Retina

Retinal laser: scatter laser (PRP) for new blood vessels in diabetic eye disease and vein blockages, and focal laser for leaks near the macula.

Other topics

Glaucoma

What glaucoma is, how it is tested and followed, and what to expect from drops, laser and surgery.

Cataract

Cataract surgery from first visit to recovery, and the laser that clears a cloudy capsule afterward.

Dry Eye

Why eyes feel dry, gritty or watery, the eyelid problems behind most dry eye, and what helps. Also watery eyes and eye allergy.

Cornea

The clear window at the front of the eye: scratches, pterygium, corneal swelling from Fuchs dystrophy, keratoconus, infections, and corneal transplants.

Vision Simulators

See what eye conditions do to everyday scenes, and what a glasses prescription means, on your own screen.
