Add each eye drop or eye-related pill, how often it is used, and which eye. To taper a drop, list it once per step with each step's stop date. The schedule lists the drops and a daily routine with suggested times, spacing drops five minutes apart; the times can be moved to fit when you wake up and go to bed. It can be printed, added to a phone's calendar with reminders, or opened on a phone with its QR code.
