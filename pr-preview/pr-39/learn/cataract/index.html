Learn›Cataract

A cataract is clouding of the eye's natural lens. These pages cover the surgery that replaces it, how to prepare, how the lens implant is chosen, and the quick laser sometimes needed afterward.

Cataract Surgery

What a cataract is, when surgery makes sense, lens implant choices, the day of surgery and the four-week recovery, and what changes if you have glaucoma.

Choosing a Lens Implant

Monofocal, toric, extended depth and multifocal lens implants for cataract surgery: what each does for far, arm's length and near vision, and the trade-offs.

Preparing for Cataract Surgery

What happens before, during and after cataract surgery: measurements, choosing a lens, medicines, the day itself, the first weeks, and when to call.

YAG Capsulotomy

YAG capsulotomy: a quick office laser that clears the cloudy capsule behind a lens implant after cataract surgery. The visit, recovery and risks.

Retina
