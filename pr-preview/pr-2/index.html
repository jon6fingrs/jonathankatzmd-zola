About Dr. Katz

Dr. Jonathan Katz is a fellowship-trained glaucoma specialist and comprehensive ophthalmologist who joined Katzen Eye Group in 2019. He treats primary and secondary glaucoma medically and surgically, from laser and minimally invasive procedures to trabeculectomy and tube shunt surgery, and performs cataract surgery. He trained at George Washington University and completed his residency and glaucoma fellowship at the Emory Eye Center.

Plain-language explanations of glaucoma, the tests used to follow it, and what to expect from treatment.

What Is Glaucoma?

Glaucoma is damage to the optic nerve, usually linked to eye pressure. Why it has no symptoms early, who is at risk, and how it is treated.

Glaucoma Testing

What happens at a glaucoma visit: eye pressure, the visual field test, OCT imaging, gonioscopy and corneal thickness, and what the results mean.

Using Eye Drops

Step-by-step technique for putting in eye drops, why to wait five minutes between drops, pressing the tear duct, missed doses, storage and refills.

SLT Laser

Selective laser trabeculoplasty: an office laser that lowers eye pressure by treating the eye's drain. What it is for, what the visit is like, and what to expect afterward.

MIGS

Minimally invasive glaucoma surgery: small implants and procedures, often combined with cataract surgery, that lower pressure with a quicker recovery than traditional surgery.

Trabeculectomy

Trabeculectomy creates a new drainage channel under the eyelid to lower eye pressure in advanced glaucoma. What the operation involves, the twelve-week recovery, and warning signs.

Tube Shunt Surgery

Glaucoma drainage devices (Ahmed, Baerveldt): a small tube and plate that drain fluid from the eye. Who they are for, the operation, and the six-week recovery.

Cataract Surgery

What a cataract is, when surgery makes sense, lens implant choices, the day of surgery and the four-week recovery, and what is different if you also have glaucoma.
