<p class="jk-page__lede">Dry eye is one of the most common reasons eyes burn, feel gritty, water, or blur on and off. These pages explain what is going on in the tear film and the eyelids, and what can be done about it. Watery eyes and eye allergy are here too, because they overlap with dry eye and are often confused with it.</p>
