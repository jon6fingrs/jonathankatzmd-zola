<p class="jk-page__lede">The retina is the light-sensing layer at the back of the eye. These pages explain the retinal conditions seen most often, which symptoms are urgent, and what treatment involves. Retinal surgery and injections are done by a retina specialist; your eye doctor will refer you when one is needed.</p>
