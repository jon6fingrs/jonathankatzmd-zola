<p class="jk-page__lede">The cornea is the clear dome at the front of the eye. It lets light in and does most of the eye's focusing, so anything that scratches, clouds or reshapes it affects vision. These pages cover the corneal problems seen most often and how they are treated. Corneal transplants, cross-linking and specialty contact lenses are provided by cornea and contact lens specialists; your eye doctor will refer you when one is needed.</p>
